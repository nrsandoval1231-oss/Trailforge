# Current opportunity screen 015

## Objective

Assess the current PSEG Long Island/LIPA Temporary Generation RFP as a possible
large-scale prime-power lead while preserving the 300 kW and source-evidence gates.

## Result

Promising current candidate, held pending the RFP document's per-project minimum
capacity and rating language. Do not promote to a verified shortlist fixture yet.

## Evidence

- Official PSEG Long Island proposals page lists the 2026 Temporary Generation RFP
  dated 2026-07-27.
- Publicly indexed summary reports up to 400 MW from multiple projects and a
  2026-10-30 response deadline.
- The public page does not establish a minimum capacity for each project or state
  prime rating, so the 300 kW prime-power classifier cannot safely qualify it.

## Allowed actions

- Preserve the candidate as a held research record.
- Retrieve and inspect the RFP documents when publicly available.

## Prohibited actions

- Outreach, registration, submission, or treating aggregate 400 MW as proof that
  every individual project is at least 300 kW.
