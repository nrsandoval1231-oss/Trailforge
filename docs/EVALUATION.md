# TrailForge Evaluation

## Primary outcome

TrailForge optimizes for **useful lead-time advantage**, not synthetic policy scores or completion of governance machinery.

For each historical project, measure time between the earliest date the policy could validly surface the project using then-available evidence and a later verified conventional/obvious milestone.

`lead_time_advantage_days = (obvious_milestone - first_valid_surface) / 1 day`

No score is produced when either timestamp is unsupported.

## Buying-event and discovery evaluation

Evaluate both historical potential and actual discovery performance. Backtracing a known project can generate a hypothesis, but only name-blind/general-source discovery earns independent discovery credit.

Preserve event date, document date, verified public-availability date, observed date, and prediction date as distinct timestamps. Hold out whole project/procurement families, keep amendments and awards with their parent case, and include time-separated, buyer-separated, geographic, negative, and unresolved examples where available.

A first actionable detection should identify a real project/site or operating need, a supported power-need hypothesis, a plausible responsible organization or buyer path, and a concrete reason to investigate or contact. It does not require perfect MW or duration when the project is still early.

Where a suitable buying event is known, also report:

`useful_lead_time = buying_event_or_obvious_milestone - first_actionable_detection`

Keep historical potential lead time separate from actual prospective/live detection lead time.

## Cohort metrics

Where evidence supports them, report median/distribution of lead-time advantage, percentage surfaced before the obvious milestone, percentage surfaced while the package remained influenceable/open, unresolved count, action burden, buyer-path availability at first surface, source coverage by signal family, research cost, and relevant-opportunity miss rate. False-positive rate requires a defensible negative cohort. Evaluate candidate policies under a comparable attention budget rather than rewarding alert volume.

Never convert UNKNOWN/inconclusive cases into negatives.

## What does not establish success

Test count, synthetic throughput, more alerts, attractive UI, one anecdotal hit, an already-public RFP, or identifying a fuel provider already aware of the project.

## Promotion

A signal family becomes a candidate for GridLens when repeated historical evidence shows material lead-time advantage with tolerable action burden and explainable provenance. Promotion is deliberate, never automatic.
