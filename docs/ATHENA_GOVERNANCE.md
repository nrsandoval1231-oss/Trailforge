# Athena governance

Athena may emit an attribution, candidate lesson, or explicit candidate policy
artifact. `govern_candidate` requires zero hard-invariant failures, robustness
passes, sealed-holdout evidence, minimum floors, and objective improvement
before promotion. Otherwise the result is rejected or quarantined.

`NegativeKnowledge` keeps rejected lessons in memory and supports hash-checked
save/load snapshots and exact-context reuse. Persistence was added in the
later local hardening history and is covered by the existing tests. No Athena
artifact self-promotes and no Trailforge artifact grants commercial or
execution authority.


## TrailForge learning target

For pre-RFP discovery, Athena may improve validated policy context around signal combinations, source priority, research order, buyer-path investigation, and alert timing. It must learn from explicit observed outcomes and UNKNOWN states without converting missing public procurement into a negative label.

Athena may propose that TrailForge investigate a different missing fact or source next, but deterministic code retains replay cutoffs, arithmetic, evaluation metrics, persistence, and promotion gates. Jev/model judgments remain inputs to evaluation rather than self-validating facts.

Historical pattern discovery is hypothesis generation. Promotion requires unseen/holdout evidence showing objective improvement at tolerable action burden. Prospective live proof remains the strongest confirmation that a policy creates real commercial lead-time advantage.
