# Historical fixtures

The registry currently contains GT-001 through GT-006. Every fixture begins as
`UNVERIFIED` and is non-scoreable. `historical_replay` permits scoreable replay
only when an operator has independently established the complete timeline and
verified historical availability, then marks the fixture `SEALED_READY`.

Current discoverability is not proof of past availability. Known outcomes must
remain invisible to earlier replay points. Small fixture counts must not be
presented as statistically significant forecasting evidence.
