# TrailForge customer-first V1 — owner direction, 2026-10-01

## Authority and objective

The owner's 2026-10-01 request establishes this product direction: identify real
power projects early enough to reach the customer before Borealis or other
fuel-led competitors. Detect project -> verify temporary/bridge/commissioning
need -> identify actual customer/buyer chain -> prepare first outreach and log
customer conversation -> only after understanding need, coordinate fuel.
Fuel vendors are downstream fulfillment partners, never required for admission.
This explicitly extends the older Arena-only product scope; it does not waive
research temporal integrity, sealed-outcome requirements, or external-action approvals.
Code establishes implemented behavior; this document establishes this extension's
requirements. Older scope documents remain evidence of their original packets.

## Opening Tribunal — once

Luna Simplifier: one local persistent project dossier and customer action queue.
Terra Engineer: SQLite transactions, separate operator adapter, existing loopback
security pattern, packaged assets and restart/security tests.
Sol Architect: preserve research engine; admit early signals before fuel/need is
known; evidence-bound buyer/need records; human conversation before fuel.
Controller decision: MODIFY product direction, PROCEED with OPERATOR-048.

Build now: local browser app, source-backed project intake, search/filter,
evidence and need review, buyer-chain/contact records, editable conversation
preparation, manual interaction log, downstream fuel record, persistence/export.
After OPERATOR-048, DISCOVER-049 adds bounded on-demand discovery from two official
project-owner feeds. Items remain unreviewed; explicit admission creates UNKNOWN
claims. Source errors, excluded off-host links and first-observed times remain visible.
Do not build yet: autonomous crawler, external sending, hosted accounts,
CRM integration, paid enrichment, forecasts, pricing or policy promotion.
Architecture: separate SQLite-backed operator module and loopback UI; no change
to Arena policy, fixtures or replay semantics. No new runtime dependencies.
Assumptions: single local operator; discovery checks Microsoft Local and QTS news
on explicit request, complemented by manual source capture. This is deliberately
narrow coverage. Source dates differ from when the app observed the record.
Risks: unsupported qualification, invented buyer authority, accidental sending,
stale project claims, local request attacks, lost updates or corrupt storage.
Sequence: bounded Luna build -> deterministic controller tests and real-source
acceptance -> independent Sol review -> bounded repairs -> exact final acceptance.

## Finish contract

1. Start the installed or source package and open a local project queue. Discover
   current official feed items, review a real project source, admit it with unknown
   need, and prove duplicates/errors/restart retain honest state.
2. Save an early project with a public source, retained excerpt and observed time;
   do not require an RFP, supplier, known fuel, or known power quantity.
3. Retain evidence and explicit UNKNOWN need, supply, timing and buyer facts.
   Verified need requires a named reviewer, evidence and appropriate power scope.
4. Add sourced owner/developer/EPC/buyer/contact roles without inventing authority.
5. Prepare a first-conversation brief and draft the operator can copy; nothing is sent.
6. Log an actual human conversation and learned MW, timing, duration and site
   constraints. No fabricated live conversation in production acceptance data.
7. Fuel coordination is blocked until verified need and an actual conversation
   with understood requirements; contradiction/unknown demotion relocks it.
8. Reopen after restart, recover records, export a dossier, surface corruption,
   reject malformed/unauthorized mutations and stale edits; preserve research gates.
9. Prove real-source intake and buyer/need evidence honestly. Prove post-conversation
   transitions with clearly segregated synthetic acceptance data. Neither proves
   live customer engagement, current buying intent, competitive lead time or forecasts.
10. Complete repository checks, package/runtime/browser proof and independent review.

Usable local V1 means the operator workflow and bounded on-demand discovery are
accepted. Broad automated monitoring, live customer response and full Arena research validation are separate
unfinished capabilities, never implied by V1 acceptance.
