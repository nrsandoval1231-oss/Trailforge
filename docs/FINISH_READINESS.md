# TrailForge — Procurement Product Readiness
Canonical October 29, 2026. Governed by [PRD](PRD.md).

The finish line is **reliable early discovery, verified actionable current RFPs/RFIs and durable expired procurement intelligence**, not a general mining-power prediction engine.

## Release gates
1. **Source coverage:** 3–5 official energy/infrastructure procurement sources; log source availability, last scan and errors; actual notices and documents rather than synthetic fixtures.
2. **Record correctness:** stable issuer/procurement ID, type, stage, status, source URL, documents, deadline timezone and last authoritative check; unknown when not proven.
3. **Lifecycle:** new, amended, extended, expired, withdrawn, canceled and awarded changes linked to immutable versions. RFI can be open for information but not for bids; expiry does not imply award.
4. **Workflow:** Discover, Live, Archive and Intelligence screens; source-backed procurement brief, supplier fit, manual tracking and material-change alerts.
5. **Archive value:** expired and awarded records retained with linked reissues, issuer history and projected recurrence clearly marked as estimated.
6. **Evaluation:** measure recall on chosen source portals, qualified lead precision, timeliness vs portal baseline, status false positives, deadline/amendment detection, user actionability and cost. Test unsupported/unreachable portals, date timezone, contradictory amendments, distinct lots, duplicate URLs, missing deadlines and document restrictions.

## Today vs target
Current local Find uses Firecrawl and deterministic RFP-like candidate ranking. It is not a verified Live monitor. Historic replay and 25 research dossiers are supporting R&D, not the blocking release acceptance test. Do not claim ready until verified live records and the lifecycle gates work end-to-end.

## Legacy historical cohort
Existing `compare-signal-families` and 20–50 project cohort methods remain available as optional historical experiments, particularly to evaluate pre-solicitation lead time. Never convert unreviewed dossiers into verified evidence. The earlier historical cohort target is **not** a prerequisite to launching useful procurement discovery and tracking.
