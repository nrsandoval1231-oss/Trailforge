## Data behavior
Early = advance notice, market research, presolicitation. Active = authoritative source-confirmed currently open RFP/RFI/RFQ/tender, with verified deadline/timezone; RFI may be open for information but not bids. Historical = expired, awarded, withdrawn, canceled, closed, preserving all revisions. Intelligence is a supporting full page, not a fourth home board. Source verification, provenance and unknowns remain visible; don't equate deadline passed with awarded.
