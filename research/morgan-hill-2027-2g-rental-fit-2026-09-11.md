# Morgan Hill 2027 Generator Services - 2G rental fit

## Evidence

Source: `2027 Generator Maintenance Services RFP.pdf`, City of Morgan Hill,
published August 6, 2026.

- Contract covers routine, preventive, emergency, and non-emergency service for
  30 city generators.
- Listed equipment ranges from 10 kW to 500 kW, including portable units mainly
  in the 150-190 kW range.
- Rental generators are an as-needed Task 4, not the primary contract scope.
- Rental weekly rates must include a fully operational unit, delivery,
  installation, maintenance, and pickup.
- Emergency response requires 24/7 availability and site arrival within two
  hours of initial communication.
- Proposal evaluation gives rental rates 3 of 30 cost points; service,
  capability, experience, and qualifications dominate.

## Fit assessment

Classify as `SERVICE_LED_RENTAL_ATTACH`, with provisional fit `MEDIUM_HIGH`.
This is a good target only if 2G can satisfy or partner for California
licensing, prevailing-wage and insurance requirements, load-bank testing,
reporting, maintenance coverage, and 24/7 response. It is not evidence of
material rental volume or of 2G qualification.

## Evidence boundary

The RFP PDF establishes requirements and the official bid page establishes the
published solicitation record. Neither establishes award, realized rental
volume, or a complete point-in-time outcome timeline. Keep the fixture
non-scoreable and do not mark it `SEALED_READY`.
