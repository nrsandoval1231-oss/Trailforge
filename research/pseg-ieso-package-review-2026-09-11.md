# PSEG and IESO procurement package review

## Decision

Neither supplied procurement is a direct 2G Energy Rental pursuit. Both are
permanent-capacity procurements outside the Aura 408 R and Aura 412 R rental
envelope. Neither identifies a temporary construction-power or
commissioning-rental package.

Keep the underlying projects only as future construction/EPC watchlists. That
does not imply a later rental package will exist. Advance only when a named
developer or EPC issues separate temporary-power scope with supported load,
timing, fuel and buyer evidence.

## PSEG Long Island 2026 PJM RFP

### Procurement scope

This is a PJM capacity or capacity-and-energy PPA, issued May 1, 2026 to
replace an expiring capacity contract. It is not the separate PSEG temporary-
generation RFP. Capacity must come from PJM projects in JCPL LDA or EMAAC,
qualify for export to Long Island/NYISO Zone K, and be delivered at the PJM
project interconnection point. The PPA requires the seller to own, operate and
maintain the proposed generating facility.

It requires at least 50 MW of contract capacity and at least 50 MW per project,
seeks 685 MW, and has a fixed June 1, 2030 through October 31, 2039 service
term. Its proposal deadline was May 29, 2026. The Q&A confirms LIPA will not
extend the term past October 31, 2039 because of its stated interpretation of
a post-2040 fossil-fuel restriction.

### 2G conclusion

**CLOSED AND DIRECTLY INCOMPATIBLE.** Aura 408 R and Aura 412 R are 480 V,
EPA non-emergency rental packages rated at 310/260 kW and 500/400 kW on
natural gas/propane. This PPA evaluates qualifying PJM capacity projects and
long-term transmission, credit, availability and operating obligations.

The 50 MW minimum is equivalent to 100 Aura 412 units at 500 kW nameplate,
but that arithmetic is not a proposal strategy and does not satisfy PJM/NYISO
qualification, interconnection/export rights, PPA obligations or the commercial
structure. Retain it only as a capacity-market reference. Do not contact the
procurement team.

## IESO Nova Scotia Fast-Acting Generation RFP

### Procurement scope

IESO Nova Scotia seeks one or two permanent facilities at Salt Springs and/or
Marshdale. The selected proponent must invest in, develop, construct, own,
operate and maintain the facility under a 25-year tolling agreement. Each
facility must have 275-300 MW maximum contract capacity and multiple generating
units so one equipment contingency does not reduce available net capacity by
more than 150 MW.

The latest addendum extends submission to January 21, 2027, but registration
remained due August 21, 2026. Direct participation required registration and a
non-refundable CAD 20,000 plus HST package fee. The confidential site,
permitting and interconnection material is not in the supplied public ZIP.

The facility must use natural gas as primary fuel and light fuel oil as
secondary fuel, transfer without a unit trip or loss of synchronization, and
store five days of liquid fuel at maximum contract capacity. It also must reach
maximum contract capacity from cold start within ten minutes, remain stable at
20% or less of it, pass 12-hour tests on each fuel and a five-day all-units
reliability test, and provide specified grid-support services.

### 2G conclusion

**REGISTRATION CLOSED AND DIRECTLY INCOMPATIBLE.** The published Aura materials
establish NG/propane ratings, not light-fuel-oil backup, seamless NG-to-LFO
transfer, five-day liquid-fuel storage, the stated grid services, Canadian
compliance or a 275-300 MW permanent facility. Meeting the lower capacity
limit would take 550 Aura 412 units on nameplate arithmetic alone, which does
not establish technical or commercial compliance.

Keep only as an EPC watchlist. The public package does not state a temporary
MW requirement, rental duration, construction-period fuel availability or a
responsible rental buyer. Do not impute a 3 MW package or budget.

## Form review

The PSEG Respondent Data Form is a capacity-offering workbook. The IESO
workbook requires proponent, project, financing, team-experience and economic-
bid data. Neither creates a smaller equipment-rental entry path.

## Next research move

Stop direct-procurement pursuit of these packages. Search selected-proponent
and EPC announcements after award, while prioritizing public tenders with an
explicit planned temporary construction, commissioning or bridge-power scope.
For every candidate, evidence load/duty, fuel, duration, integration and buyer
before sizing Aura equipment.
