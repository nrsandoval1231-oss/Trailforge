# 2G Aura ICP RFP discovery - 2026-09-11

## Search result

The search found technically relevant natural-gas microgrid solicitations, but
no currently actionable RFP was confirmed in this pass.

### High technical fit, response closed

- **178th Wing Base Microgrid Construction, Springfield, Ohio** - SAM Notice
  W50S8Q-26-B-A001. Base bid includes natural-gas generators and microgrid
  controls; the public notice describes critical 24/7 operations and a $7M-$9M
  construction magnitude. Offers were due August 3, 2026, and the notice became
  inactive August 18, 2026. This is a strong Aura-scale pattern but not an
  active lead.
- **Joint Base McGuire-Dix-Lakehurst Microgrid, New Jersey** - SAM Notice
  W912DS26BA032. The presolicitation describes two 500 kW natural-gas
  generators, PV, BESS, and a microgrid controller. The listed response date
  was August 1, 2026, with inactive date August 16, 2026. Strong technical fit;
  not actionable now.

### Scale mismatch

- **PSEG Long Island 2026 Temporary Generation RFP** - official PSEG source
  describes up to 685 MW of capacity or capacity plus energy, with a May 29,
  2026 proposal deadline. This is a utility-scale procurement, not an Aura
  408/412 unit-scale target.
- **IESO Nova Scotia Fast-Acting Generation RFP** - official procurement is for
  one or two 300 MW natural-gas facilities. Exclude as far outside the product
  envelope.

## Search filter update

Prioritize active notices with explicit natural-gas generation or microgrid
scope, two or more units near 300-500 kW, critical-load or resilience use, and
an open response date. Exclude closed notices, utility-scale generation above
the Aura deployment pattern, generic diesel maintenance, and emergency-service
contracts.
